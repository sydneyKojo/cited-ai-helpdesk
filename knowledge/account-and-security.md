# Account and security

## Reset your password
Click "Forgot password" on the login page and enter your email. The reset link is valid for 30 minutes. If the email does not arrive within 5 minutes, check your spam folder.

## Two-factor authentication
Turn on two-factor authentication under Profile → Security using any authenticator app. Admins on the Scale plan can require it for the whole team.

## Export your data
Admins can export all customers, invoices and payments as CSV files from Settings → Export. Large exports are emailed as a download link that expires after 7 days.

## Delete your account
Account deletion is permanent and must be confirmed by the account owner. Contact support to request it; a person will verify your identity before anything is deleted.

## Data storage
Data is stored in the EU and encrypted at rest and in transit. Backups are kept for 30 days.

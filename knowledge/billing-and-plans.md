# Billing and plans

## Plans and prices
Starter costs $12 per month and includes 1 user and 50 invoices per month. Growth costs $29 per month and includes 5 users, unlimited invoices and automatic payment reminders. Scale costs $79 per month and includes unlimited users, API access and priority support. Paying yearly saves 20%.

## Change your plan
Admins can change plan from Settings → Billing → Change plan. Upgrades take effect immediately and you are charged a prorated amount for the rest of the billing period. Downgrades take effect at the end of the current billing period.

## Cancel your subscription
Admins can cancel from Settings → Billing → Cancel subscription. Your account stays active until the end of the period you have paid for. After that it becomes read-only for 90 days so you can export your data, and is then deleted.

## Refunds
Monthly plans are not refunded for partial months. Yearly plans cancelled within 30 days of purchase get a full refund. Refund requests are handled by the billing team, not by the help assistant; contact support and a person will review it.

## Payment methods and receipts
We accept Visa, Mastercard and American Express. Receipts are emailed to the billing contact after each charge and are also available under Settings → Billing → Receipts.
